We study whether a world-action model can be built on a frozen predictive visual encoder without inheriting a complete pretrained visual generator. V-JEPA Policy learns a future predictor and action expert on that latent space, which also supports transferring future-modeling knowledge from DROID videos and instructions to robot control.

Key findings

We study what a WAM needs to inherit, how its visual foundation affects robustness, and whether the same V-JEPA latent space supports acquiring and transferring future-modeling knowledge.

Finding 1. A frozen predictive visual encoder is a sufficient foundation for WAM learning.

The future predictor and action expert are learned jointly from downstream demonstrations on frozen V-JEPA 2.1 latents. Without inheriting a complete pretrained visual generator, the 0.9B From Scratch policy reaches 97.25% on LIBERO and 50.92% on RoboCasa-GR1.

Finding 2. Predictive latents provide the strongest robustness under a shared downstream recipe.

With the policy backbones, demonstrations, and training budget held fixed, V-JEPA 2.1 ViT-L leads the tested encoders on LIBERO-Plus. Its advantage over DINOv2 widens from 2.35 points on LIBERO to 12.23 points under distribution shifts.

Finding 3. V-JEPA latent space supports acquiring and transferring future-modeling knowledge.

The same frozen V-JEPA 2.1 encoder defines the latent inputs and future-prediction targets in DROID pretraining and downstream policy learning. A predictor pretrained without action labels transfers its future-modeling knowledge through its weights to joint training with a new action expert, raising LIBERO-Plus success from 79.25% to 91.50% at the same downstream budget.

Learning and transfer in V-JEPA latent space (Figure 2)

Learning a WAM on a frozen visual foundation.

The From Scratch design tests the sufficiency of predictive visual latents directly. The pretrained visual encoder stays frozen, while robot demonstrations jointly train a new future predictor and action expert. Their layer-wise context key–value states connect future modeling to action generation; language and proprioception condition both modules.

Freeze a predictive space

V-JEPA 2.1 ViT-L encodes each camera view into spatiotemporal tokens. The encoder is shared across pretraining and policy learning and never receives downstream gradients.

Forecast future latents

Instruction-conditioned future queries predict the next latent tubelets in one forward pass. Context states become future-informed through bidirectional interaction.

Generate an action chunk

A flow-matching action expert attends to the predictor's layer-wise context key-values and produces a 32-step action chunk without rendering imagined pixels.

Learning and transfer in V-JEPA latent space.

A shared V-JEPA 2.1 encoder maps current observations and future frames into the same frozen latent space in both stages. On DROID videos, instructions and observed state condition a predictor trained to forecast future latents, without action labels or an action expert. Its learned weights then initialize the downstream predictor, which supplies future-informed context to a newly initialized action expert during joint policy learning. The latent space thus supports both direct WAM learning and the acquisition and transfer of broader future-modeling knowledge.
